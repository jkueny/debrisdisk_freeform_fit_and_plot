### What do we want to get out of the freeform model?
+ The dust distribution of the disk is complex, with the effects of KLIP+ADI it's hard to measure directly.
  - Computing the freeform forward model allows retrieval of an approximation of the true dust distribution.
  - The freeform model still fits speckle noise and WDH artifacts...
+ It's kinda circular to fit a model to the freeform model to study the dust.
  - We are seeing asymmetric brightness, maybe a changing radius for the disk spine, and
  definitely different levels of decay as we going around in azimuth.

### We DO want to fit a physical disk model to the freeform model
+ The only way to extract the disk parameters out of the freeform model, with error.
+ There's value in comparing the best-bit parameters of the freeform model vs. diskFM results.

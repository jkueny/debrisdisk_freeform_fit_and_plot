* Learning rate
As far as I can tell, the learning rate just affect the convergence time for a given run.
** Large learning rate
+ Only tried the default l_r=1e-1, which still gives good results.
+ Going larger will take less iterations to converge...
  - At the expense of possibly missing the global minimum.
** Small learning rate
+ Increases the number of iterations needed to converge to a minimum loss value.
+ Tends to lead to overfitting, however, balance with lambda_reg
